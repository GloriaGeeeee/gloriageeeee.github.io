About me

Gloria Yunhan Gao

I started out as an engineer — and kept getting distracted by the person on the other end of the code.

That distraction turned into the work. Side projects first, then design at IBM, then the whole career: web and mobile products that aren't just beautiful, but that hold up when real people use them.

Today I'm a Senior Product Designer at Pepperstone, working on partner platforms. Before that, financial products at OKX — forty of them, from first look through to subscription.

I'm endlessly curious about how different industries work, and about the human behaviour running underneath them.
